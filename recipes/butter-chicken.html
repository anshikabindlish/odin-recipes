Butter Chicken

Description

Butter chicken is a rich and creamy Indian dish made with tender pieces of chicken cooked in a spiced tomato and butter-based sauce.

It is flavorful, comforting, and delicious when served with rice or naan.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Butter
  • Tomatoes
  • Cream
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Garam masala
  • Chili powder
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Cook the chicken until lightly browned.
  3. Cook the onion, garlic, and ginger in butter.
  4. Add the tomatoes and spices and cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add the cooked chicken and simmer in the sauce.
  6. Stir in the cream and cook for a few more minutes.
  7. Serve hot with rice or naan.
