Gulab Jamun

```

Description

Gulab jamun is a popular Indian dessert made from soft milk-based dough that is shaped into small balls, cooked until golden, and soaked in a sweet, fragrant syrup.

The dessert is traditionally flavored with cardamom and rose water and is often served warm or at room temperature.

Ingredients

  • Milk powder
  • All-purpose flour
  • Baking powder
  • Milk
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Cardamom
  • Rose water
  • Cooking oil or ghee for frying
  • Pistachios or almonds for garnish

Steps

  1. Mix the milk powder, flour, and baking powder.
  2. Add ghee and enough milk to form a soft dough.
  3. Shape the dough into small, smooth balls.
  4. Prepare a sugar syrup with water, sugar, and cardamom.
  5. Heat the oil or ghee over medium-low heat.
  6. Carefully cook the dough balls until they are evenly golden brown.
  7. Transfer the cooked gulab jamun into the warm sugar syrup.
  8. Let them soak in the syrup so they become soft and flavorful.
  9. Garnish with chopped pistachios or almonds and serve.
