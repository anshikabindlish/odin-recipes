Biryani

Description

Biryani is a flavorful Indian rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, aromatic spices, and seasoned chicken. The ingredients are layered and cooked together so that the rice absorbs the rich flavors of the spices and chicken.

It is commonly served with raita and is a popular dish for family meals and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Garam masala
  • Turmeric
  • Chili powder
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Fresh mint

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices.
  3. Cook the onions until golden brown.
  4. Add the chicken, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, and spices.
  5. Cook the chicken until it is mostly done.
  6. Partially cook the basmati rice separately.
  7. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture.
  8. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice and chicken are fully cooked.
  9. Garnish with fresh cilantro and mint before serving.
